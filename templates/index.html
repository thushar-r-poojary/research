ADAT — Agentic Dual-Attention Transformer

Novel dual-image SR architecture using shared RCAB encoders and Spatial-Pooled Cross-Attention Fusion to align sub-pixel information from two slightly shifted low-resolution satellite captures.

  • Shared RCAB encoder with channel attention
  • Spatial-Pooled Cross-Attention Fusion (SPCAF)
  • PixelShuffle upsampler (×3 scale)
  • Global bicubic residual learning
  • 2 input feeds → 1 HR output
